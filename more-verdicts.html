Representative Cases

  • UNINSURED MOTORIST/DRUNK DRIVER - LOW BACK SURGERY- St. Lucie County Circuit Court Trial - May, 2009

    $534,693.84 verdict for a 50-year old female in case against an uninsured drunk driver and against the uninsured motorist insurance carrier, Nationwide.

  • UNINSURED MOTORISTNECK INJURY REQUIRING SURGERY- St. Lucie County Circuit Case (2000 trial)

    $265,350 verdict for female client injured by uninsured driver. Initial defense offer $6,000.00. State Farm ultimately paid $530,000 to settle all claims, including bad faith in May, 2000.

  • PRODUCT LIABILITY/SHOULDER NJURY TO PARALYZED MALE- St. Lucie County Circuit Court - 2002

    Settlement with a present value of $1.6 million in a products liability case against the distributor of a defective wheelchair. Litigated in Federal and State Court.

  • BREACH OF TRUST AGREEMENT- St. Lucie County Circuit Court - 2002

    Pretrial settlement in the amount of $1 million in a breach of trust case on behalf of the beneficiary against the trustee. The case included multi-state discovery in Ohio, Mississippi and Florida.

  • HEALTH INSURANCE BAD FAITH/PARALYSIS- St. Lucie County Circuit Court - 1998

    Pretrial settlement of $1.75 million in an insurance bad faith case against a health insurance carrier which had wrongfully refused to pay for medical bills of $850,000 for severely injured female accident victim. Litigated in both State and federal Court as co-counsel.

  • AUTO COLLISIONNEGLIGENT HIRING AND ENTRUSTMENT - Delia vs. Design Concepts and Romano, St. Lucie County Circuit Court (1986 trial)

    $350,000 jury verdict (collected) for female client who suffered two broken knees and a broken jaw in an auto accident. Owner of corporation found personally liable for negligence for hiring a driver with a terrible driving record.

  • SKULL FRACTURE - NUM CHUCKS - St. Lucie County Circuit Court (1992 summary trial)

    $191,500 (collected) settlement after verdict for 22 year old male injured in an attack by num-chucks sold to a minor child by the defendant store.

  • DRUNK DRIVER AUTO CRASH/INSURANCE BAD FAITH/PUNITIVE DAMAGES - St. Lucie County Circuit Court (1996 trial)

    $98,500 verdict for female client injured by a drunk driver. Pretrial offer $25,000. Post trial the bad faith case against Allstate settled for over $177,000.

  • TRACTOR TRAILER COLLISION

    2006 Settlement ($1.68 million) for injured dump truck driver struck in rear b tractor-trailer on I-95.

  • CRASHWORTHINESS/WRONGFUL DEATH- Indian River County 1982

    Settlements against drunk driver, uninsured motorist insurance company and against Toyota for constructing and selling a defective/uncrashworthy vehicle. The case involved a deceased mother of two who was killed when a drunk motorcycle driver crashed his bike into a Toyota she was driving. Total settlement pretrial of over $1 million. The young lady's mom later became President of Florida M.A.D.D.

  • AUTO CRASH/INSURANCE BAD FAITH

    2007 St. Lucie County jury verdict of $326,000 (upheld on appeal) for male construction worker. Neck surgery after herniated disc injury. Collected 3 times the insurance coverage of $100,000.

  • POLICE OFFICER INJURED/DRUNK DRIVER

    2008 Settlements against drunk driver and UM carrier totaling $350,000 for Port St. Lucie Police Officer severely injured in collision with drunk driver.

The above cases are indicative of the types of cases handled but are not an indication of the value of your specific case. Each case is unique and must be evaluated by taking all individual factors into account.
