Representative Cases

  • WRONGFUL DEATH - U.S. District Court, 2019

    Verdict of over $5 million for the wrongful death of an 18-year-old female student killed in a head-on collision with a 99-year-old driver traveling the wrong way on a highway with lights off. Prior to trial, we also secured a $500,000 settlement with the passenger in the at-fault vehicle.

  • AUTO CRASH/INSURANCE BAD FAITH - 2019

    St. Lucie County jury verdict of $267,608.88, which was affirmed on appeal in 2026. Despite an initial defense offer of $4,350, Progressive ultimately paid in excess of $354,000 to settle all claims, including bad faith, in March 2026.

  • PREMISES LIABILITY - 2023

    Okeechobee County Circuit Court confidential settlement with HCA Raulerson Hospital for a client who suffered injuries after being thrown from a wheelchair while visiting the facility.

  • AUTO CRASH - Martin County Circuit Court, 2023

    $275,000 settlement for a female client who sustained significant chest and back injuries.

  • AUTO COLLISION (SEMI-TRUCK) - St. Lucie County Circuit Court, 2023

    Judgment in excess of $350,000 after verdict for a female client injured in a collision with a semi-tractor trailer on I-95. This was the highest verdict in St. Lucie County that year.

  • LOW BACK INJURY - Indian River County Circuit Court, 2025

    $140,000 settlement for a female client who suffered a low back injury in an automobile collision.

  • WRONGFUL DEATH - Orange County Circuit Court, 2025

    Settlement on behalf of a minor child for the wrongful death of an employee who was crushed by a crane while working in Orange County, Florida.

  • SHERIFF'S DEPUTY INJURED - 2025

    Settlement against an at-fault driver and UM carrier for a St. Lucie County Sheriff's Deputy who was severely injured in a vehicle collision.

The above cases are indicative of the types of cases handled but are not an indication of the value of your specific case. Each case is unique and must be evaluated by taking all individual factors into account.
